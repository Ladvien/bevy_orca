## Iteration 1 — 2026-10-30 — the crowd bench, and a neighbour grid sized by the crowd

**What changed.** Built `examples/bench.rs` (section 2's instrument, backlog #1), and its first run found the item: `Crowd`'s `HashMap<(i32, i32), Vec<usize>>` grid allocated whenever a warm crowd walked into new ground (25 804 allocations in 480 steps of the 10 000-agent circle swap), its per-step clear grew with the ground covered, and a member at a far finite position (`x = 1e30`) overflowed `cx + 1` and panicked in a debug build. The grid is now one sorted `Vec<(cell key, member)>` rebuilt every solve (row-major key, a row of the 3×3 block is one range found by two binary searches, neighbouring cells in `i64` clamped to the `i32` range). Every solved velocity is bit-identical. Also fixed: the crate cited HRVO's DOI (`10.1109/TRO.2011.2120810`) for the ORCA paper; it is `10.1007/978-3-642-19457-3_1` (`paper_get` confirmed both).

**Why it ranked first.** Issues: none open (`gh issue list` empty). No parked branch or `WIP(pass` ref. Among the bugs the bench found, this one breaks a documented promise ("`Crowd` allocates nothing after its buffers are warm") and panics on caller data, and its fix is provably behaviour-neutral (hashes). Runner-up: the NaN outputs on non-finite agent/neighbour state (README: "the result is always finite") — also a bug, but which answer an unusable own state gets is a design decision that needs its own research; it is next. Third: the 20-agent circle-swap deadlock.

**Sources.** Craft: `10.1109_robot.2008.4543489` (neighbour region; only neighbour selection is superlinear), `10.1103_physreve.51.4282`, `10.1109_tro.2011.2120810`, `10.1073_pnas.1016507108`. Shipping: Recast/Detour `DetourCrowd` (`dtProximityGrid` sized from `m_maxAgents`, cleared every update; GitHub, not in the corpus), `gameaipro3-ch19-rvo-orca-how-they-really-work`, `s2008-advances-full-course-notes-4`.
